Why you can trust Tijori

Your family's IDs are the most private thing on your phone. Here is how Tijori looks after them.

1

Works offline

Scan, search, copy and get reminders with no connection.

2

Locked to you

Your documents are locked on your phone. Your Vault Password, or your own fingerprint, opens them.

3

Stays on your phone

No account and no server, so your documents never leave your phone unless you send them. A backup is a file you keep wherever you like, and only your password opens it.

    Questions

    Is it safe to install an app from outside the Play Store?

    Android warns about every app that doesn't come from the Play Store; it doesn't mean the app is harmful. Every Tijori release is signed with the same key, so you can check that the app you install is genuine.

    Does it cost anything?

    Tijori is free today, with no ads.

    What happens if I lose my phone?

    Your documents are encrypted, so whoever finds the phone can't read them without your password. To get them back on a new phone, keep a backup: in Tijori, go to Settings, then Backup, and save the encrypted backup file to Google Drive or anywhere you like.
